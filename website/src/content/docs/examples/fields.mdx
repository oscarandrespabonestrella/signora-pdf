A **field** is a named area of a page that gets a signature. See
[viewer options](../../guides/viewer-options/#signature-fields) for every detail.

## Fill and validate

Signatures can only go in the fields (`[freePlacement]="false"`). Press **Validate** before and after
signing the required field.

<Example name="fields" title="Signature fields" height={560} />

## Lay out the fields

`fieldsMode="edit"` is for whoever prepares the document: add, name, move and resize fields. Store
the JSON from `(fieldsChange)` and pass it as `signatureFields` to the people who sign.
